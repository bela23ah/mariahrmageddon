Features

Everything the Christmas Survival Center can do.

  • Earmuff Run: Mini game: survive 24 days among the sound waves.
  • Burglar Defence: Idle clicker: defend a big house from two burglars with Christmas traps.
  • Tower Builder: Stack the floors: a won tower gets a sign on top.
  • Christmas Bingo: Tick off what you live through over the holidays, from family arguments to the news.
  • Places to avoid and safe places: A map of where Christmas music is likely playing and where it is calm.
  • Friend teams: Create a team with friends or colleagues and see who lasts longer.
  • Town contest: Which town has the most survivors?
  • Collective goal: If 10% of registered players are still in by Christmas, everyone wins.
  • Advent calendar and holiday props: A daily door, an advent wreath and a gift tree with collectible props.
  • XP, levels and badges: Days survived earn badges, and you collect XP while playing.
  • Elimination report and postcard: If the song got you, report it and share the postcard.
  • Map: Where players fell and where they still survive.
