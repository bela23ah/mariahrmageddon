3. What others can see

Your nickname can be seen by others on leaderboards and in your teams (if you allow it in your profile; otherwise you appear as “Hidden survivor”). The public map and summaries show only town-level totals, never names. Nobody can see your e-mail address.

4. Processors and recipients

  • Google Ireland Limited (Firebase Authentication, Firestore), Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers: reminder e-mails and the help assistant).
  • Brevo: sending the “Still alive?” e-mails.
  • Google (Gemini API): automatic answers to questions typed into the Help window. On the free tier of the Gemini API, Google may use submitted content to improve its products, so please do not type personal data into the question. The question is sent without your identifier or e-mail address.

Some providers may process data outside the EU (for example in the USA) on the basis of standard contractual clauses (SCC).

5. Location data

The browser passes on your position only with your permission, which you can withdraw at any time. For an elimination we store a location accurate to at most ~1 km.

6. Local storage and cookies

The site uses the browser's local storage (localStorage) for settings, caching and sign-in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

You can also write to kecskemeti.fiatalok.tarsasaga@gmail.com; we answer within one month. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling. You may lodge a complaint with the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (naih.hu, Falk Miksa utca 9–11, 1055 Budapest, ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu) or with a court, or with your local data protection authority.
