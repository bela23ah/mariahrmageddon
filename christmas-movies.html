Song-free Christmas movies

Which Christmas movie can you watch without getting Wham'd? 98 movies without the original Mariah Carey, Wham! or José Feliciano recording, based on the official Wham-Safe Viewing list of whamageddon.com and IMDb/Tunefind music credits.

Note: only the original artist's original recording counts; covers do not. The data can be incomplete, so mistakes can happen. Only watch movies you know.

Covers (do not count)

  • Love Actually (2003): The Mariah Carey song is performed by Olivia Olson, so it is a cover and does not count.
  • Christmas with the Kranks (2004): A “Feliz Navidad” cover (Davie Allan and the Arrows, Steven Van Zandt): does not count.
  • The Christmas Chronicles: Part Two (2020): A “Feliz Navidad” cover (Mariachi Plata): does not count.
