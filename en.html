What does it mean to get Wham'd?

You heard the original “Last Christmas” by Wham! after December 1. You're out. Same goes for Mariah Carey's “All I Want for Christmas Is You” (you got Carey'd Away) and José Feliciano's “Feliz Navidad”.

The three fronts

  • Mariahrmageddon: Mariah Carey, “All I Want for Christmas Is You” (1994).
  • Whamageddon: Wham!, “Last Christmas” (1984).
  • Feliznavidageddon: José Feliciano, “Feliz Navidad” (1970). Invented by the Kecskemét Youth Society.

Rules

  1. Only the original recordings count. Covers and remixes are fine.
  2. The game runs from December 1 to midnight on December 24.
  3. Recognise it, even from one bar, and you are out. Own up and share it.
  4. Two modes: Traditional (one front after another) or Die Hard (all three at once).

Where does it come from?

Whamageddon was started by Danish friends in the mid-2000s and took off after its Facebook page launched in 2016, according to CBS News. The Mariah variant is also played abroad as Mariahpocalypse. In Hungary the Kecskemét Youth Society runs it as Mariahrmageddon.
